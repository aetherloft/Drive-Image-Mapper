Privacy Policy

Image Mapper runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own and keeps no copy of your files or account data.

What the app accesses

The app asks for the drive.file scope, which limits it to files you open with Image Mapper, files you choose in the Google file picker, and the .imap map files the app creates. It cannot list or read anything else in your Drive. It also asks for drive.install, which lets Google Drive show Image Mapper in the “Open with” and “New” menus.

What the app stores

Each image map is saved as an .imap file in your Google Drive, next to the image when you have permission to add files there, otherwise in My Drive. The app also writes the map file’s ID into a private app property on the image so it can find the map again. You can delete map files at any time from Drive.

The demo mode stores its sample map in your browser’s local storage only.

Sharing

Image Mapper never changes who can see your files. Drive sharing settings apply as usual.

Third parties

Requests go directly from your browser to Google (sign-in, Drive API and Google Picker). Google’s own privacy policy covers those services.

Revoking access

Remove the app at any time from your Google Account permissions or from Drive settings under Manage apps.
